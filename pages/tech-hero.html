Tech Hero: Catt Small

Catt Small is a video game developer and product designer living in New York. She first began programming when she was 10, in order to create her own dress-up dolls. This early activity reflects her current career straddling the boundaries of code and art, as well as her studies in Graphic Design and Integrated Digital Media. Small has contributed to projects building websites and video games, through a wide variety of avenues. Beyond contributing to the code itself, Small also contributed through design and UI/UX.

You can see Catt Small's prowess simply by a glance at her personal website and appreciate her meticulous efforts with further investigation. Her website responds to different screen sizes; her layouts enable more columns on larger screens and repositions them for smaller screens. Small's custom mouse design contributes to her memory-book theme and shows her attention to detail. One of the most exciting aspects of her website, however, is the little animations. They are subtle enough that they are not distracting, but they truly bring the website to life. Just watch her companion character animate in the top-left corner of the various pages, and you will appreciate the charm! Lastly, check out the biography section of the About page. It is truly hilarious that Small allows users to choose how long her biography should be! It feels like a commentary on the attention span of people today.

Besides being a skilled coder and designer, Catt Small is also an avid leader. Small has shared her knowledge with many learners. She organized monthly events for newbies in design and technology for about three whole years. Small even made contributions to minorities in a few ways. For example, Small helped to create and run Code Liberation, a non-profit organization focused on teaching gender minorities how to program video games. Additionally, Small helps organize events for Game Devs of Color Expo, which provides a platform for people of color to showcase their skills and projects. Furthermore, Small helped create Good for PoC, which helped people of color find supportive work environments in tech for two years, before donating its remaining funds to help give people of color opportunities in tech.

Catt Small is proof that a typical trajectory is not necessary for success. Her personal timeline documents numerous ways she has contributed to various projects, whether for the simple joy of improving user experience or for the pleasure of making the world a better place. In fact, her impact may be more far-reaching than the work of employees at big tech companies. That's what makes her so inspiring. Most coders aren't headed for big tech, so seeing someone like Small create real change matters. It gives the rest of us hope that we can matter, too.
